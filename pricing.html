Billing FAQ

How does billing work?

You are billed monthly or yearly depending on your plan. Payments are processed securely. You can cancel anytime.

Can I upgrade or downgrade?

Yes! You can change your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately with prorated billing.

Do unused credits roll over?

Credits reset each billing cycle and do not roll over. Use all your credits before the cycle ends.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee for new subscriptions. Contact support for refund requests.
